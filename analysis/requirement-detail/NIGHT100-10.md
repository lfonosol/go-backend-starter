# NIGHT100-10 — Dispatch at day's end

> "Work is dispatched at the end of the working day and runs unattended overnight, replacing a cycle bounded by working hours."

MoSCoW and Status live in the shared Scope Tracker sheet, and the requirement's phase in `.planning/ROADMAP.md` — **look them up there; this card does not restate them.** The quotes below are the workshop-corpus record.

**Sources:** change record `CR-090` · locus `S-01 25:16`

## What this is for

At present the amount of work a team gets through is bounded by how many hours a person is awake to supervise it, and someone has to stay present pressing buttons and keeping a machine running. This requirement breaks that link: work is handed over at the end of the day and continues without anybody there. The delivery team named it as the single largest win available to them, because it turns the overnight hours into delivery time at no extra staffing cost. It is also the rhythm every other requirement in the delivery half assumes.

Those are separate rules with their own rows. This requirement builds the rhythm; the night requirements bound it.

**RAID rows linked to it.** None. No row on the RAID sheet links this requirement.

**Glossary terms in play.** *Daily sprint* — a one-day cycle: demo at noon, feedback turned into issues and specs, work dispatched at about four, agents run overnight, review in the morning. *Overnight mode* — one of four named modes of the practitioner's run loop. *Harness*.

## Open items and watch-outs

- **A dispatch is only as safe as the bounds around it**, and those are four separate requirements. Shipping this one alone gives an unattended run with nothing stopping it.
- This requirement is the root of the whole delivery half. Nothing that writes into the sixth stage can land before it exists.
- The decision log the morning review reads is written by this run, so its format is a contract between this requirement and the morning one, not a detail inside either.
