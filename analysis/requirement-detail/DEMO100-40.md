# DEMO100-40 — Hand over for UAT

> "The pack carries a UAT handover for what was built, short enough for a business tester who holds a day job to act on."

MoSCoW and Status live in the shared Scope Tracker sheet, and the requirement's phase in `.planning/ROADMAP.md` — **look them up there; this card does not restate them.** The quotes below are the workshop-corpus record.

**Sources:** change record `CR-230` · locus `S-02 the daily session pack` and `S-01 40:54`

## What this is for

Showing a client what was built is not the same as letting them try it, and until they try it nobody knows whether it works for them. This part of the pack hands the business something they can act on the same day. The length constraint is the substance rather than a nicety: the testers are business people with their own jobs, and a test document that is expensive to read simply does not get used, which means the feature is never tested at all. What it buys is feedback within a day instead of at the end.

## The evidence

**What the source says.**

> "However that we can streamline and give clear instruction on the test, and with sort of the minimum time they spend they can test it out, so they can quickly also give the feedback to you guys to log."
> — a delivery-team participant, `S-01 40:54`

The same speaker states the constraint a few seconds earlier: the business testers **"still have their day job"** (`S-01 40:38`), and **"we don't want to overwhelm them of how complicated the test of the feature on that day is"** (`S-01 40:48`).

**The reasoning on the register.** **The part no row carried before.** The register held the demonstration and the planning of what comes next, and separately a rule about the size of a test document, but nothing said the session hands the business something to test with. **A test document that is expensive to read is a feature that does not get tested.**

**Judgment calls that bear on it.** **Both loci stand because a row was absorbed rather than deleted.** A separate process stage carried a row about the size of a test document; its substance was folded into this row and that stage now carries none. That is why the decomposition still lists eight process stages while only seven carry requirements — **the absence is declared, not an omission.** **A plan that treats the size constraint as a nice-to-have has dropped the only thing the struck row contributed.**

**Glossary terms in play.** *Daily sprint*. *Vertical slice* — what makes a day's build testable as a whole path rather than as part of a layer.

## Open items and watch-outs

- **This requirement can succeed while the capability fails.** The document can exist and be short, and the testers can still not have the time. **That is outside the repository's gift**, and the two must be kept apart rather than letting a passed check imply a working loop.
- **The size constraint is the substance of an absorbed row**, so it cannot be traded away as a refinement.
- Feedback arriving daily needs somewhere to go. Nothing in this row says where.
