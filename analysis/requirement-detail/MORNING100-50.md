# MORNING100-50 — Capture the lessons

> "The morning review ends with a lessons-learned step whose agreed output is written into the repository, and which may be promoted from this engagement to the harness itself."

MoSCoW and Status live in the shared Scope Tracker sheet, and the requirement's phase in `.planning/ROADMAP.md` — **look them up there; this card does not restate them.** The quotes below are the workshop-corpus record.

**Sources:** change record `CR-180` · locus `S-01 41:53`

## What this is for

A team running a daily loop learns something almost every day, and almost all of it evaporates. This requirement catches it at the one moment when it is fresh and someone is already reading the night's work, and writes the agreed version into the repository rather than into a memory. The second half is the part that compounds: a lesson can be lifted out of this engagement and into the harness, so the next engagement starts from it. That is the mechanism by which the way of working improves across clients rather than only within one.

**The reasoning on the register.** **The promotion path is explicit and is what makes the harness improve across engagements rather than within one. A lesson stays with the project unless it is promoted.** The row changes the pipeline document and the lessons section of both prompt kits' handoffs — **so the promotion is a real mechanism in the kit, not an intention.**

**Judgment calls that bear on it.** The row was read against the question *does it produce an artifact whose contents the practitioner would pick?* and left silent on interactivity, for a stated reason: its own statement already reads *whose agreed output is written into the repository*, and **"agreed" carries the same guarantee the interactive clause carries, in a word the row already has.**

**RAID rows linked to it.** None. No row on the RAID sheet links this requirement.

**Glossary terms in play.** *Morning report*. *Harness* — the pod's own tooling and process, which is what a lesson is promoted into.

## Open items and watch-outs

- **Promotion is a decision, not an automatic step.** Nothing here says who decides that a lesson leaves the project, and a promoted lesson reaches every future engagement.
- The step sits at the end of a review that may already be long. If the review runs out of attention, this is the part that gets skipped.
- Writing the lesson into the repository is easy; finding it again later is the part nothing here addresses.
