# MORNING100-40 — Rank by criticality

> "Entries in the morning review are classified by criticality and the highest-value ones are read first."

MoSCoW and Status live in the shared Scope Tracker sheet, and the requirement's phase in `.planning/ROADMAP.md` — **look them up there; this card does not restate them.** The quotes below are the workshop-corpus record.

**Sources:** change record `CR-170` · locus `S-01 26:57`

## What this is for

The more work is pushed to the machine, the longer the morning's list becomes, and at some volume one person cannot read it. Ordering the list by how much each entry matters means the reviewer can stop reading part-way through and still have seen everything that could hurt. That is what keeps a single human viable as the supervisor of an ever-larger amount of unattended work — which the session named as the binding constraint on how fast the whole thing can go. It is load-bearing rather than cosmetic: without it the review does not scale and the person becomes the bottleneck.

- **There is nothing to rank by yet.** No severity classification exists. The same vocabulary is needed by the night's second exit bound, and deciding it once, earlier, is the cheaper order.
