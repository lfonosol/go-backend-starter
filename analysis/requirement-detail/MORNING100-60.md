# MORNING100-60 — Log the problems

> "Problems encountered are logged systematically as they occur, for use during the engagement and at its retrospective."

MoSCoW and Status live in the shared Scope Tracker sheet, and the requirement's phase in `.planning/ROADMAP.md` — **look them up there; this card does not restate them.** The quotes below are the workshop-corpus record.

**Sources:** change record `CR-190` · locus `S-01 40:03`

## What this is for

The team has been arriving at retrospectives and trying to recall what went wrong over the preceding weeks, which reliably produces the loudest problems rather than the most frequent ones. Writing each one down when it happens replaces recall with a record, and the record is useful twice: during the engagement, where a repeating problem becomes visible early enough to fix, and at the end, where the retrospective has evidence instead of impressions. It costs a few seconds at the moment of annoyance and it is the only requirement here aimed at how the team improves rather than at what it delivers.

## The evidence

**What the source says.**

> "Baking in the logging of those problems that occur, whether they're in the autonomous part or if they're in like our general processes — we should really log those issues that we have so we can use them either during the project or then in a retrospective identify the pain points more systematically, because we've kind of had to remember them from, you know, just the top of our heads."
> — the delivery lead, `S-01 40:03`

**The reasoning on the register.** **Requested directly, with the reason given in the same breath: the team had been relying on memory to recall pain points at retrospectives.** The row creates a problems file in the repository the receiving team builds in, reached by that repository's own front door and by the delivery kit's declared-outputs section. **That path is a file this work creates, not a pointer into this package.**

**Judgment calls that bear on it.** The row was left silent on interactivity for the same stated reason as the decision log: **it is written by a process as it runs, so there is no moment at which a human picks its contents.**

**RAID rows linked to it.** None. No row on the RAID sheet links this requirement.

**Glossary terms in play.** *Harness*. *Daily sprint* — the one-day cycle inside which problems arise.

## Open items and watch-outs

- **Logging at the moment of annoyance is a habit, not a mechanism.** Nothing here makes it happen; if it is easier to carry on than to write the line, the log stays empty and the retrospective is no better off.
- The scope is deliberately wide — the autonomous work and the general process both — so the file will mix kinds of problem unless something separates them.
- The destination is a file in the receiving team's repository, not in this package. A reader following the path from here will not find it.
