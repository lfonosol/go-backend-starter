# DEMO100-20 — Demonstrate the overnight build

> "The pack carries a demonstration of what the overnight run built."

MoSCoW and Status live in the shared Scope Tracker sheet, and the requirement's phase in `.planning/ROADMAP.md` — **look them up there; this card does not restate them.** The quotes below are the workshop-corpus record.

**Sources:** change record `CR-210` · locus `S-02 the daily session pack` and `S-01 30:30`

## What this is for

Work that ran overnight is only real to a client when they see it. This part of the pack is where the night's output becomes visible: it shows the client what now exists, every day, which is what makes a fast delivery rhythm credible rather than merely claimed. The judgment it depends on is which of the night's output is worth showing — the planning data records what was built, and says nothing about what a client would want to look at, so a person chooses. Without this the loop produces work and no confidence in it.

## Open items and watch-outs

- **A night that built nothing demonstrable still produces a session.** Nothing here says what the demonstration is on a day when the run stopped on a gate.
- The choice of what to show is a judgment made daily, so the quality of this part depends on the person, not the mechanism.
- It depends directly on the dispatch, which is why this work is sequenced after the night and the morning rather than beside them.
