# SITE100-20 — Clean the content pages

> "Every content page on the handover site is reviewed and given an explicit organisation, page by page."

MoSCoW and Status live in the shared Scope Tracker sheet, and the requirement's phase in `.planning/ROADMAP.md` — **look them up there; this card does not restate them.** The quotes below are the workshop-corpus record.

**Sources:** change record `CR-030` · locus `S-02 capture`

## What this is for

The handover site is the thing a client actually opens, and at the moment its pages carry whatever the generator happened to produce rather than a deliberate shape. This pass goes through every content area and decides what each one is for and how it should read, so the site becomes something designed rather than something emitted. The reader it changes things for is the client, who meets a page that has been thought about. It is also the pass that produces the decisions the other two site requirements depend on, which is why it runs first and cannot be run in parallel with them.

## The evidence

**What the source says.** All three of this phase's requirements come from one passage, the first capture in the practitioner's notes.

**Glossary terms in play.** *Progressive reveal* — a presentation device on the handover site that exposes the additional context made available to the AI beneath what a human reader consumes. *Role-based view* — a view selected by the reader's role.

## Open items and watch-outs

- **The per-area decision is the output of this pass, not an input to it.** A plan that asks for the answer before the work starts has the order backwards, and that is the phase's hard constraint.
- **The marking may not be a binary.** If an area needs a human-facing summary with model context beneath it, then it carries a pair rather than one value, and the configuration field the marking rule adds takes a different shape. Settle that here, because the next requirement builds on the answer.
- A defect travels alongside this work and is not a requirement: the same capture reports that in mobile mode the navigation overlaps the phase diagram and the roadmap. It describes the shipped template, so it is present on every engagement built from that template.
